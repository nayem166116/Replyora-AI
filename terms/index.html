Overview

Replyora is committed to making customer support clearer and more useful. This page explains how information is handled when you visit our website, contact our team or use Replyora services.

Information we collect

We may collect information you provide directly, such as your name, work email, company details and messages sent through our contact forms. We may also collect basic technical information such as browser type, device information and pages visited.
