Cookie Policy

How Replyora uses essential storage and optional cookies across our website.

Last updated: September 13, 2026
What we useEssential storageOptional storageYour choicesContact

What this policy covers

This Cookie Policy explains the storage technologies used by the Replyora website. It should be updated if analytics, advertising, chat, payment or other third-party tools are added.

Essential storage

Essential browser storage supports basic site behaviour such as remembering cookie preferences, keeping the navigation experience consistent and storing the email address temporarily during the email-verification flow. Essential storage cannot be disabled through the preference panel when the related feature requires it.

Optional storage

Optional analytics and marketing storage are not required for the website to function. They should remain off unless you choose them through the banner or Cookie settings panel. The current website does not claim that optional analytics or advertising tools are active.

Your choices

You can accept all, reject non-essential storage, or manage optional categories from the cookie banner. You can reopen Cookie settings from the footer. You can also delete stored data through your browser settings.

Contact

For questions about cookies, use the Replyora contact form.
