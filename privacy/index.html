Privacy Policy

How Replyora collects, uses and protects information when you use our website and services.

Last updated: September 13, 2026
ScopeInformationUseRetentionRightsContact

Scope

This Privacy Policy explains how Replyora handles information when you visit this website, contact the team, use the public Reply Composer, or evaluate Replyora for your organisation. It is written for the current website experience and should be updated when production services or data processors are added.

Information we collect

Information you provide

We may receive your name, work email, company name, support context and any message you submit through a contact or account form.

Information collected automatically

Our website may receive basic technical information such as browser type, device type, approximate region, referring page and pages visited. The public Reply Composer keeps the message you enter in the browser for the composition session; do not enter confidential customer data.

How we use information

We use information to operate the website, respond to enquiries, provide requested product information, protect the service, understand website usage and improve the product experience. We do not sell personal information.

Retention and sharing

We retain information only for as long as needed for the purpose it was collected, to meet legal obligations, resolve disputes or protect the service. We may use carefully selected infrastructure providers to operate the website. Before production customer data is processed, Replyora should publish the relevant provider list and data-processing terms.

Your choices

You can request access, correction or deletion of information you have provided. You can reject optional cookies through the cookie banner and reopen your choices from the footer. Essential storage may still be required for site functionality.

Contact

For privacy questions, use the Replyora contact form. Confirmed privacy contact details should be added before production launch.
